# Lessons from Hijrah: Embracing Change for a Better Future

**Author:** Muhammed Navar Kavanur  
**Publication:** *Insight Magazine* · Alathurpadi Dars Students Association (ADSA)  

---

In the year 622 CE, history was forever split. The greatest leader mankind has ever seen travelled from Makkah to Madīnah. Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ and his companions left everything they had—their houses, families, and wealth. That was not an escape from enemies out of fear, but a well-planned, masterful migration that no one but him ﷺ could execute. There are plenty of lessons men and women have to learn, especially in this era.

## Embracing Change

After the revelation at the age of forty, the Prophet ﷺ initially propagated Islam to his family and friends. Firstly, with a small number of companions, the propagation of Islam was conducted privately.

The conversion of Ḥamzah ibn ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib ﵁ and ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb ﵁ was a turning point in the propagation of Islam. The private propagation turned into public propagation with the grand march towards Kaʿbah Sharīf led by Ḥamzah ﵁ and ʿUmar ﵁. For the Quraysh, that was an unexpected move from the Muslims. Because they lost two of their courageous youth resources, and then Muslims started public propagation as a challenge. They strengthened their hatred and violence towards Muslims. Prophet ﷺ and his companions did not even take a single step back from their objective.

These were the hard times of the believers, but they trusted the plan of Allāh ﷾. Allāh ﷾ says in the Holy Qurʾān:

<div dir="rtl" lang="ar">

> ﴿وَإِذْ يَمْكُرُ بِكَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ لِيُثْبِتُوكَ أَوْ يَقْتُلُوكَ أَوْ يُخْرِجُوكَ ۚ وَيَمْكُرُونَ وَيَمْكُرُ ٱللَّهُ ۖ وَٱللَّهُ خَيْرُ ٱلْمَـٰكِرِينَ﴾ [[سورة الأنفال: 30](https://quran.com/8/30)]

</div>

> *"Remember how the unbelievers plotted against you, to keep you in bonds, or slay you, or get you out, and Allāh too plans; but the best of the planners is Allāh."*

After thirteen years of private and public propagation in Makkah, the Hijrah of the Prophet ﷺ started to Madīnah. Even though this was not the first migration in Islam, the Madīnah migration was the first Hijrah of the Prophet ﷺ and the major one in Islamic history. Some of the companions had already migrated to Abyssinia to propagate Islam.

The Hijrah was not an overnight occurrence. Prophet ﷺ met the people of Madīnah in the season of Ḥajj in the previous year of Hijrah. That accord is known as the ʿAqabah Accords. Asʿad ibn Zurārah (the leader of the Khazraj tribe), Rāfiʿ ibn Mālik (the Aws tribe leader), ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿAmr, and others met the Prophet ﷺ in a place called ʿAqabah and sent Muṣʿab ibn ʿUmayr ﵁ to Madīnah. He worked hard to make the land of Madīnah ready to accept the Messenger of Allāh ﷾ and his companions.

The people of Madīnah were very eager to welcome the Prophet ﷺ. The companions started to migrate group by group privately. Prophet ﷺ was waiting for the command of Allāh ﷾. The remarkable migration was the one by ʿUmar ﵁. He migrated publicly by challenging the Quraysh.

The news of the migration reached the leaders of Quraysh. They met at Dār al-Nadwah and planned to kill the Prophet ﷺ. They announced a hundred red camels as a reward for the one who would kill the Prophet ﷺ. They appointed men from each tribe.

Prophet ﷺ and ʿAlī ibn Abī Ṭālib ﵁ were inside the Prophet's house. The enemies were waiting for the Prophet ﷺ outside his ﷺ house. Allāh ﷾ allowed the Prophet ﷺ to leave Makkah. He ﷺ asked ʿAlī ﵁ to sleep in his bed. Then he ﷺ threw a handful of dust towards the enemies and went to the house of Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq ﵁. Then they both took the way to the cave of Thawr. Quraysh followed them and reached the top of the cave. The friend Abū Bakr ﵁ asked the Prophet ﷺ, "Won't they see us if they just look at their feet?" The Prophet ﷺ replied with what is the highest Tawakkul to the Creator. Allāh ﷾ mentions this situation in the Holy Qurʾān:

<div dir="rtl" lang="ar">

> ﴿إِلَّا تَنصُرُوهُ فَقَدْ نَصَرَهُ ٱللَّهُ إِذْ أَخْرَجَهُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ ثَانِىَ ٱثْنَيْنِ إِذْ هُمَا فِى ٱلْغَارِ إِذْ يَقُولُ لِصَـٰحِبِهِۦ لَا تَحْزَنْ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَنَا ۖ فَأَنزَلَ ٱللَّهُ سَكِينَتَهُۥ عَلَيْهِ وَأَيَّدَهُۥ بِجُنُودٍۢ لَّمْ تَرَوْهَا وَجَعَلَ كَلِمَةَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ ٱلسُّفْلَىٰ ۗ وَكَلِمَةُ ٱللَّهِ هِىَ ٱلْعُلْيَا ۗ وَٱللَّهُ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٌ﴾ [[سورة التوبة: 40](https://quran.com/9/40)]

</div>

> *"If you help not (your leader), (it is no matter): for Allāh did indeed help him, when the unbelievers made him leave: he had no more than one companion; they two were in the cave, and he said to his companion, 'Have no fear, for Allāh is with us': then Allāh sent down His peace upon him, and strengthened him with forces which you saw not, and humbled to the depths the word of the unbelievers. But the word of Allāh is exalted to the heights: for Allāh is exalted in might, Wise."*

They stayed there for three days. Then continued their journey. They took an unusual path along the coast of Ḥijāz so that Quraysh would not notice. Surāqah followed them and reached almost close to the Prophet ﷺ, but the feet of his horse got buried in the sand thrice as direct help from Allāh ﷾. Then they reached Qubāʾ, the nearest place before Madīnah. They built a masjid there and moved to Madīnah. The people of Madīnah were waiting for them all day after the news of the departure. And the day arrived. Girls welcomed their beloved with daff and singing:

<div dir="rtl" lang="ar">

> طَلَعَ البَدْرُ عَلَيْنَا ... مِنْ ثَنِيَّاتِ الوَدَاعْ  
> وَجَبَ الشُّكْرُ عَلَيْنَا ... مَا دَعَا لِلَّهِ دَاعْ  

</div>

> *(O the Full Moon has risen over us  
> From the valley of Wadāʿ  
> It is an obligation to show gratefulness  
> When a caller calls to Allāh ﷾)*

The companions were then known as 'Muhājirūn' and 'Anṣār'. The migrants from Makkah to Madīnah were later known as 'Muhājirūn', and the people of Madīnah who welcomed the migrants were known as 'Anṣār'. The 'Anṣār' shared everything they had with their brothers and sisters—from their wealth and business, and even offering to divorce a spouse so their Muhājir brother could marry them honourably.

## The Fruits of the Hijrah

Then, what did they earn after all this pain and effort for years?  
Made an unerasable mark for Islam, built the first Islamic state including non-believers, conquered Makkah eight years after Hijrah, spread Islam to Baghdad, Iran, Isfahan, Toledo, Seville, Cordoba, Egypt, Spain, Europe and Malaya.

Even after the Conquest of Makkah (Fatḥ Makkah), the Prophet ﷺ stayed till his death. Allāh ﷾ completed this religion in the land of Madīnah. History has never recorded anything like the growth of Islam. Islam reached across the world from the desert of Arabia in just 23 years.

In the era of the Khilāfah of ʿUmar ﵁, when they discussed about the need for a calendar, they settled on the great event, the Hijrah.
