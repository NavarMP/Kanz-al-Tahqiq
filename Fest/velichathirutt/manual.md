## PART I: ADMINISTRATIVE FRAMEWORK & GENERAL REGULATIONS

### 1. Constitutional Authority & Governance
* **Advisory Board Sovereignty**: The Advisory Board represents the supreme legislative and constitutional authority of the fest. Any amendment, alteration, or addition to these rules requires their formal approval.
* **Fest Committee**: The executive body entrusted with operational execution, scheduling, logistics, and enforcement.
* **Official Notifications**: Any post-publication policy modifications will be communicated exclusively through official Fest Committee letterheads signed by the Advisory Board.
* **Adjudication Integrity**: The decisions of the independent Jury Panel are sovereign and final. Participants and team leaders are strictly prohibited from approaching judges directly without express prior authorization from the Fest Committee.
* **Fest Identification**: Every registered student must hold and carry their official Fest Number card at all times. In the event of card loss, a replacement fee of ₹20 applies.
* **Institutional Discipline**: All students and houses are bound by oath to maintain the decorum, moral uprightness, and spiritual sanctity of the Dars.

### 2. Student Batch Categorisation
The student body is categorized into four competition tiers:
1. **Sub-Junior**: 1st Batch (First Year Dars students).
2. **Junior**: Batches 2, 3, and 4.
3. **Senior**: Batches 5, 6, and 7.
4. **General**: Open category encompassing students across all batches.

### 3. Participation Ceilings & Team Quotas
| Category / Type | Individual Limit | House Quota / Limits | Special Provisions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Non-Stage (Individual)** | Maximum 10 items | Min 3 entries per house; no upper ceiling | Allows broad participation |
| **Stage (Individual)** | Maximum 4 items | Min 3, Max 5 entries per house | Specific exemptions apply |
| **General (Individual)** | Maximum 4 items | Maximum 3 entries per house | Combined Stage & Non-Stage ceiling |
| **Sub-Junior Category** | **No upper ceiling** | No upper ceiling | Open participation in Stage & Non-Stage |
| **Group Competitions** | As per item rules | Pre-registration of all Fest Numbers mandatory | Team points credited to house score |

> [!NOTE]
> **Stage Individual Exemptions**: The following individual stage competitions are exempt from the individual 4-item ceiling: Qira'at (Quran Recitation), Hifdh (Memorisation), Prose Reading, Grantha Reading, Pada Payattu (Word Chat) Arabic, Pada Payattu English, and Prompting.

### 4. Scoring Matrix & Point System
Points are allocated across individual and group competitions according to the official matrix:

| Placement / Rank | Individual Item Points (Student & House) | Group Item Points (House Total Only) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **1st Place (First Prize)** | **5 Points** | **15 Points** |
| **2nd Place (Second Prize)** | **3 Points** | **10 Points** |
| **3rd Place (Third Prize)** | **1 Point** | **5 Points** |

* Individual placements contribute directly to both the student's individual championship tally and the house aggregate score.
* Group placements contribute exclusively to the house championship aggregate.

### 5. Official Calendar & Registration Deadlines
* **Non-Stage Registration Closes**: Tuesday, 27 October 2026 at 11:00 PM.
* **Stage Registration Closes**: Tuesday, 10 November 2026 at 11:00 PM.
* **Verification & Scrutiny Date**: Tuesday, 17 November 2026.
* *Strict Adherence*: The registration portal will close automatically at the scheduled time; late registrations will not be entertained under any circumstances.

### 6. Non-Stage Examination Protocols
* **Time Management**: Candidates must be seated inside the examination hall strictly on time. No extra time is allotted for late arrivals.
* **Absolute Anonymity Protocol**: Answer scripts must bear **ONLY the candidate's Fest Number**. Writing personal names, batch numbers, house names, or identifiable marks on the answer sheet results in immediate disqualification.
* **Stationery**: Official writing papers and answer sheets will be provided by the Fest Committee. Candidates must bring their own pens, pencils, and allowed art instruments.

### 7. Stage Performance & Adjudication Rules
* **Punctuality & Stage Calls**: Participants must report to the stage call desk when announced. Unexcused absence upon call forfeiture results in opportunity loss.
* **Stage Clashes**: In the event of simultaneous schedules across parallel stages, house leaders must coordinate with the registration desk to adjust appearance order.
* **Unique Presentation Mandate**: In speech and vocal competitions, participants from the same house are strictly forbidden from delivering identical speeches, songs, or tunes. Every participant must present a unique piece.
* **The 60% Minimum Duration Rule**: Every stage performance must achieve at least **60% of the prescribed maximum time limit**. Performances falling short of 60% are disqualified from score evaluation.
* **Overtime Penalty**: A penalty of **2 marks will be deducted for every 10 seconds exceeded** beyond the maximum time limit. In Qira'at, candidates are allowed to conclude the ongoing ayah.

### 8. Appeals Mechanism & Dispute Settlement
* **Filing Window**: Formal appeals must be lodged strictly within **30 minutes** of official result declaration.
* **Appeal Fee**: A non-refundable fee of **₹100** must accompany each official Appeal Form.
* **Fee Refund Policy**: If the appeal is upheld upon committee review and the decision is rectified, the ₹100 fee will be refunded in full.
* **Finality**: All disputes are scrutinized by the Fest Committee in consultation with the Advisory Board, whose verdict is sovereign and final.
* **Disqualification Powers**: The Fest Committee retains the prerogative to disqualify individuals or entire houses for behavioral misconduct, indiscipline, or rule violations.

### 9. Disciplinary Code, Penalties & Negative Scoring
* **Universal Participation Mandate**: Every enrolled student must participate in at least one item (Stage or Non-Stage). Non-compliance results in a **-2 point deduction** from the house score and a **₹50 personal fine**.
* **Unexcused No-Show**: Registered candidates who fail to appear for an event without valid justification incur a **-2 point deduction** from the house score and a **₹20 personal fine**.
* **Authorized Leave Exemption**: Medical or personal leave requires an official Leave Form endorsed by the Advisory Board prior to the event.
* **Academic Malpractice & Collusion**: Any detected irregularity, plagiarism, impersonation, or cheating conducted with house awareness will result in an immediate **25% deduction of the house's total accumulated points**.

### 10. Apex Honors & Championship Accolades
1. **Kala Prathibha (The Apex Laureate)**: Awarded to the student in Junior or Senior categories securing the highest cumulative individual points across Stage and Non-Stage competitions. General individual points are included. In case of a tie, General group points decide the laureate.
2. **Emerging Talent**: Awarded to the outstanding individual champion in the Sub-Junior category.
3. **Sarga Prathibha (Creative Virtuoso)**: Awarded to the student securing the highest cumulative points in non-stage literary, creative writing, and fine arts competitions across Junior and Senior categories.
4. **Nadhimul A'lam (Special Distinction Award)**: Special honor bestowed for exemplary organizational and performance merit.
5. **Rolling Championship Trophy**: Awarded to the house achieving the highest aggregate points tally across all 193 events.
