## 0. Read This First (اقْرَأْ هٰذَا أَوَّلًا)

- **Purpose:** The munadhara wing exists to grow our Arabic, confidence and manners of argument. We aim to speak beautifully, listen carefully, and respect the other team. If we win, *Alhamdulillah*; if not, we still gain.
- **How the pack works:** Every Arabic line (with *tashkīl*) is followed by an English line in italics. Memorize in chunks of 3–4 lines. Where you see `[pause]`, stop and look at the audience.
- **Timing:** Each speech is about 350–400 Arabic words. At a calm pace with pauses this is about 5–6 minutes; the Bonus lines fill the rest of the 7 minutes. Practise with a timer.
- **Verify before Tuesday:** Please check every verse number, hadith wording and source with your ustad. Hadith gradings and numbers in the secular facts (marked *verify*) should be double-checked. Arabic is written simply on purpose; your ustad may polish it.

### 📅 Four-Day Preparation Plan (خُطَّةُ الأَيَّامِ الأَرْبَعَةِ):
- **Fri (الجُمُعَةُ):** Read and understand your speech thoroughly. Focus on the vocabulary and core argument.
- **Sat (السَّبْتُ):** Memorize the first half of your speech. Focus on rhythm and natural delivery.
- **Sun (الأَحَدُ):** Memorize the second half, record yourself on audio/video, and listen back to critique pronunciation.
- **Mon (الاثْنَيْنِ):** Full mock debate with the entire team, strict timer discipline, and simulated cross-examination questions.

### 🛡️ Our Golden Rule in the Whole Debate (قَاعِدَتُنَا الذَّهَبِيَّةُ):

> **«اقْبَلِ الخَوْفَ، وَصَحِّحِ الهَدَفَ» (Accept the fear, correct the target).**  
> We never say *"there is no danger."*  
> Instead, we firmly state:  
> **“The danger is real, but it comes from misuse, and the answer is wisdom and regulation, not rejecting a great blessing.”**  
> This framing makes our team virtually impossible to counter, because we acknowledge reality without yielding our thesis.
