# 05. بِطَاقَةُ الرُّدُودِ السَّرِيعَةِ وَعِبَارَاتُ المُنَاظَرَةِ (Rebuttal & Phrases)
## التَّفْنِيدُ المُوجَزُ · حَرَكَةُ التَّقَاطُعِ · المُصْطَلَحَاتُ الإِلْقَائِيَّةُ

---

## 7. Quick Rebuttal Card (بِطَاقَةُ الرُّدُودِ السَّرِيعَةِ)

| مَا يَقُولُونَهُ (Their Anticipated Point) | رَدُّنَا فِي جُمْلَةٍ وَاحِدَةٍ (Our Decisive One-Line Counter) | الدَّلِيلُ الشَّرْعِيُّ أَوِ العَقْلِيُّ (Reference) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **“Jobs will be lost”** | Jobs change, skills must grow; seeking knowledge is an obligation upon every Muslim. | Hadith Ibn Majah: 224; Speech 2 |
| **“Fake news and deepfakes”** | Crime of unethical people; the Quran’s divine cure is *Tabayyun* (verification) and regulatory law. | Surah Al-Hujurat 49:6 |
| **“Humans will stop thinking”** | The same was said by Socrates about writing; the Quran repeatedly commands: “Will you not reflect?” | Plato's *Phaedrus*; Surah Al-An'am 6:50 |
| **“Wars and autonomous weapons”** | Iron has great might and immense benefit together; moral choice lies in human statecraft. | Surah Al-Hadid 57:25 |
| **“Wine had benefits but was forbidden”** | We accept the scale of weighing! In wine, harm is intrinsic and greater; in AI, benefit is vastly greater and harm is misuse. | Surah Al-Baqarah 2:219 |
| **“Corruption appeared by what people earned”** | The verse itself specifies: *“Hands of people”* — not machines or algorithms! | Surah Ar-Rum 30:41 |
| **“Do not follow what you have no knowledge of”** | Exactly! Verify AI outputs; this sacred verse mandates rigorous verification, never abandonment of science. | Surah Al-Isra 17:36 |
| **“Even scientists warn”** | Warning equals divine prudence (*Khudhu hidhrakum*) and safety governance; it is not a call to renounce science. | Surah An-Nisa 4:71 |

### 🤝 Common-Ground Move (حَرَكَةُ التَّقَاطُعِ المُشْتَرَكِ):
<div dir="rtl" lang="ar">

> «نَتَّفِقُ مَعَكُمْ أَنَّ هُنَاكَ مَخَاطِرَ، لٰكِنَّ الحَلَّ التَّنْظِيمُ لَا الرَّفْضُ.»

</div>

> *"We agree with you that there are real risks, but the mature solution is regulation, not rejection."*  
> *(This maneuver disarms the opposition, preserves scholastic dignity, and establishes our team as the balanced voices of wisdom).*

## 8. Useful Debate Phrases (عِبَارَاتٌ نَافِعَةٌ لِلْمُنَاظَرَةِ)

### 🎙️ Opening, Transitions & Evidence:
- <div dir="rtl" lang="ar">«نَبْدَأُ بِاسْمِ اللهِ» *(We begin in Allah’s name)*</div>
- <div dir="rtl" lang="ar">«دَعُونِي أُوَضِّحُ» *(Let me clarify)*</div>
- <div dir="rtl" lang="ar">«بِاخْتِصَارٍ» *(In short / To summarize)*</div>
- <div dir="rtl" lang="ar">«الدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذٰلِكَ» *(The proof of that is...)*</div>
- <div dir="rtl" lang="ar">«مِثَالٌ عَلَى ذٰلِكَ» *(An example illustrating this...)*</div>
- <div dir="rtl" lang="ar">«أَيْ...» *(That is to say...)*</div>

### 🤝 Scholarly Courtesy & Cross-Examination:
- <div dir="rtl" lang="ar">«احْتِرَامًا لِرَأْيِكُمْ، لٰكِنْ...» *(With due respect to your perspective, yet...)*</div>
- <div dir="rtl" lang="ar">«هٰذَا سُؤَالٌ مُمْتَازٌ» *(That is an excellent question)*</div>
- <div dir="rtl" lang="ar">«نَتَّفِقُ مَعَكُمْ فِي... وَنَخْتَلِفُ فِي...» *(We agree with you regarding... but diverge on...)*</div>
- <div dir="rtl" lang="ar">«جَزَاكُمُ اللهُ خَيْرًا» *(May Allah reward you with goodness)*</div>
- <div dir="rtl" lang="ar">«مَا شَاءَ اللهُ» *(As Allah wills)*</div>
- <div dir="rtl" lang="ar">«أَسْتَأْذِنُكُمْ» *(With your kind permission)*</div>

---

### 🏆 Post-Result Etiquette (أَدَبُ إِعْلَانِ النَّتِيجَةِ):
*(Say this regardless of the judges' decision):*
<div dir="rtl" lang="ar">

«الحَمْدُ لِلهِ عَلَى كُلِّ حَالٍ، وَنُهَنِّئُ الفَرِيقَ الآخَرَ، وَقَدْ تَعَلَّمْنَا كَثِيرًا.»

</div>

*“Praise be to Allah in every circumstance; we congratulate our brothers in the opposing team, and we have learned immensely.”*
