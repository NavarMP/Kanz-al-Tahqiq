## 🎯 المَبْدَأُ الأَسَاسِيُّ (The Central Thesis)

<div dir="rtl" lang="ar">

> «الذَّكَاءُ الِاصْطِنَاعِيُّ أَدَاةٌ، وَالأَدَاةُ تَأْخُذُ حُكْمَهَا مِنْ نَفْعِهَا وَمِنْ يَدِ صَاحِبِهَا؛ وَنَفْعُهُ أَكْبَرُ، فَهُوَ نِعْمَةٌ.»

</div>

> *“AI is a tool; a tool takes its value from its benefit and from the hand that holds it. Its benefit is greater, so it is a blessing.”*

### القَاعِدَةُ الذَّهَبِيَّةُ (The Golden Rule):
> **«اقْبَلِ الخَوْفَ، وَصَحِّحِ الهَدَفَ» (Accept the fear, correct the target).**  
> We never deny the risks. We declare: *“The danger is real, but it resides in human misuse, and the solution is regulation and ethics, not rejecting a great blessing.”*
