## 9. Delivery Tips for Beginners (نَصَائِحُ الإِلْقَاءِ لِلْمُبْتَدِئِينَ)

1. **Stop at the end of each sentence (*Waqf*):** Pronounce the last letter without its vowel (with *sukūn*). This avoids grammatical ending slips (*I'rab*) under pressure and sounds polished and natural in classical oratory.
2. **Slow is strong:** Speak at roughly 70% of your casual conversational speed; a large hall or audience of 200+ requires cognitive space to absorb the Arabic diction.
3. **Use the `[pause]` marks:** Look directly at the adjudicators and audience, breathe deeply, and smile gently. Two seconds of intentional silence makes the succeeding sentence resonate powerfully.
4. **Memorize by meaning first, then words:** Say the English line in your mind, then retrieve the vocalized Arabic phrasing from the conceptual foundation.
5. **Qur'an verses and Hadith:** Recite them deliberately and with utmost spiritual reverie; master their exact *makhārij* and vocalization with your ustad beforehand.
6. **If you forget a line:** Do not freeze or show panic. Express the next core concept in simple, composed Arabic. A straightforward thought spoken with poise is far superior to silence.
7. **Hands and eyes:** Count arguments on your fingers (*Awwalan, Thaniyan, Thalithan*); keep your prompt cards low, or hold single-word mental cues.
8. **Manners win hearts (*Adab*):** Greet warmly, express gratitude, and never mock. Demolish the argument, cherish the brother.
9. **Deliberate practice cycle:** Read aloud 3 times $\to$ cover the Arabic and recall from English $\to$ record your audio $\to$ critique pronunciation $\to$ run complete mock turn with timer.

## 10. Sources to Check With Your Ustad (المَصَادِرُ لِلْمُرَاجَعَةِ)

### 📖 The Holy Qur'an (الْقُرْآنُ الْكَرِيمُ):
- [Al-'Alaq 96:1–5](https://quran.com/96/1-5): The first revelation honoring the pen, reading, and human learning.
- [Al-Baqarah 2:31](https://quran.com/2/31): Allah teaching Adam all the names (capacity for symbolic cognition).
- [Al-Baqarah 2:164](https://quran.com/2/164) & [Ali 'Imran 3:190–191](https://quran.com/3/190-191): Signs in cosmic order for people of intellect and contemplation.
- [Al-Baqarah 2:219](https://quran.com/2/219): The divine mīzān of weighing sin and utility (*Ithm* vs. *Manāfi'*).
- [An-Nisa 4:58](https://quran.com/4/58): Command to render trusts to their owners (data integrity, privacy & trust).
- [An-Nisa 4:71](https://quran.com/4/71): *“Take your precautions”* (divine command for proactive risk management).
- [Al-Ma'idah 5:32](https://quran.com/5/32): Preserving one life is as saving all humanity (AI medical detection & healthcare).
- [Al-An'am 6:50](https://quran.com/6/50): *“Will you not reflect?”* (human intellectual agency).
- [Yusuf 12:47–49](https://quran.com/12/47-49): Prophet Yusuf's 7-year strategic economic planning, grain preservation, and resource forecasting.
- [An-Nahl 16:43](https://quran.com/16/43): *“Ask the people of knowledge if you do not know”* (fatwa requires qualified scholars).
- [Al-Isra 17:36](https://quran.com/17/36): Epistemological verification across hearing, sight, and heart.
- [Ta-Ha 20:114](https://quran.com/20/114): *“My Lord, increase me in knowledge.”*
- [Ar-Rum 30:41](https://quran.com/30/41): Corruption appeared due to *what human hands earned* (moral accountability lies with human actors).
- [Az-Zumar 39:9](https://quran.com/39/9): Are those who know equal to those who do not know?
- [Al-Jathiyah 45:13](https://quran.com/45/13): All that is in the heavens and the earth subjugated to humanity.
- [Al-Hujurat 49:6](https://quran.com/49/6): *Tabayyun* — the Quranic imperative to verify reports before judgment.
- [Al-Hadid 57:25](https://quran.com/57/25): Iron sent down with mighty power and vast benefits for people.

### 📜 Prophetic Hadith (الْحَدِيثُ النَّبَوِيُّ الشَّرِيفُ):
- **Sahih al-Bukhari:**
  - *Hadith 1:* Actions are judged by intentions («إِنَّمَا الأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ»).
  - *Hadith 893:* Each of you is a shepherd and responsible for his flock («كُلُّكُمْ رَاعٍ وَكُلُّكُمْ مَسْؤُولٌ عَنْ رَعِيَّتِهِ»).
  - *Hadith 2072:* Earning through the labour of one's own hands («مَا أَكَلَ أَحَدٌ طَعَامًا قَطُّ خَيْرًا مِنْ أَنْ يَأْكُلَ مِنْ عَمَلِ يَدِهِ»).
  - *Hadith 5678:* Allah did not send down a disease without its cure («مَا أَنْزَلَ اللهُ دَاءً إِلَّا أَنْزَلَ لَهُ شِفَاءً»).
- **Sahih Muslim:**
  - *Hadith 1907:* Actions judged by intentions.
  - *Hadith 1829:* Shepherding and moral responsibility.
  - *Hadith 2699:* The path of knowledge leads to Paradise («مَنْ سَلَكَ طَرِيقًا يَلْتَمِسُ فِيهِ عِلْمًا سَهَّلَ اللهُ لَهُ بِهِ طَرِيقًا إِلَى الجَنَّةِ»); and divine aid accompanies mutual brotherhood («وَاللهُ فِي عَوْنِ العَبْدِ مَا كَانَ العَبْدُ فِي عَوْنِ أَخِيهِ»).
- **Sunan al-Tirmidhi:**
  - *Hadith 2517:* “Tie it, then place trust in Allah” («اعْقِلْهَا وَتَوَكَّلْ»).
  - *Hadith 3599:* Du'a for beneficial knowledge («اللَّهُمَّ انْفَعْنَا بِمَا عَلَّمْتَنَا وَعَلِّمْنَا مَا يَنْفَعُنَا وَزِدْنَا عِلْمًا»).
- **Sunan Ibn Majah:**
  - *Hadith 224:* Seeking knowledge is an obligation upon every Muslim («طَلَبُ العِلْمِ فَرِيضَةٌ عَلَى كُلِّ مُسْلِمٍ»).
  - *Hadith 2340:* No harm and no reciprocating harm («لَا ضَرَرَ وَلَا ضِرَارَ»).
- **Musnad Ahmad:**
  - *Hadith 2865:* Legal axiom of non-harm («لَا ضَرَرَ وَلَا ضِرَارَ»).
- **Al-Mu'jam al-Awsat (Al-Tabarani):**
  - *Hadith 6026:* The most beloved people to Allah are those most beneficial to humanity («أَحَبُّ النَّاسِ إِلَى اللهِ أَنْفَعُهُمْ لِلنَّاسِ»).

### 🌐 Secular History, Philosophy & Science (Verified Facts):
- **Melvin Kranzberg's Laws of Technology (1986):** First Law: *"Technology is neither good nor bad; nor is it neutral."* The human intent, design, and institutional context govern the outcome.
- **Francis Bacon (*Meditationes Sacrae*, 1597):** *“Scientia potentia est”* — Knowledge itself is power. Power requires ethical steering, not fearful suppression.
- **Plato (*Phaedrus*, c. 370 BCE):** The dialogue of Socrates on writing: King Thamus cautioned that writing would erode human memory; yet text became the primary preserver of civilization.
- **Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi (c. 780–850 CE):** Father of algebra and algorithmic computation; the direct namesake of *Algorithm*.
- **Badi' al-Zaman al-Jazari (1206 CE):** *The Book of Knowledge of Ingenious Mechanical Devices*, documenting sophisticated autonomous automata.
- **Nobel Prizes 2024:**
  - *Chemistry:* Demis Hassabis and John M. Jumper (Google DeepMind) for AlphaFold protein structure prediction across 200M+ proteins.
  - *Physics:* John J. Hopfield and Geoffrey E. Hinton for foundational breakthroughs in artificial neural networks.
- **Geoffrey Hinton & 2023 Statement on AI Risk:** In May 2023, Hinton stepped down from Google to advocate for AI safety guardrails. In May 2023, CAIS published the statement on extinction-scale AI risk, demonstrating that pioneers urge governance, not technological shutdown.
- **World Economic Forum (WEF) Future of Jobs Report:** Affirms structural transformation where new technological roles outpace obsolete positions.
