# Kitāb al-Ajnās al-Kubrā (كتاب الأجناس الكبرى)
### *A Comprehensive Treatise on Classical Arabic Morphology & Morpho-Semantics*
*Author: al-Shaykh Dādā ʿAlī ibn al-Shaykh Qāsim (d. 1127 AH / 1715 CE)*  
*Discipline: ʿIlm al-Ṣarf (Arabic Morphology) · Kanz al-Taḥqīq Library*

---

## Overview

**Kitāb al-Ajnās al-Kubrā** represents the senior masterwork of **Dādā ʿAlī ibn Qāsim** in Arabic morphology. Expanding upon the pedagogical framework established in *al-Ajnās al-Ṣughrā*, this treatise provides an exhaustive analysis of verbal morphology rooted in semantic transitivity (*al-Lāzim wal-Mutaʿaddī*), the classical six doors of trilateral verbs, anomalous patterns (*al-Shādh*), and the complete 20-term derivation chains.

## Advanced Philological Features

1. **Semantic Foundations (*Lāzim vs. Mutaʿaddī*):**
   - The opening chapter grounds morphology in syntax and semantics: determining which verbal weights accommodate passive construction (*Mabnī lil-Majhūl*) and objective participles (*Ism al-Mafʿūl*).
2. **The 6 Canonical Doors & Anomalies (*al-Shādh*):**
   - Deep analysis of medial radical vowel shifts (*Fatḥu Ḍamm, Fatḥu Kasr, Fatḥatān, Kasru Fatḥ, Ḍammu Ḍamm, Kasratān*).
   - Analysis of anomalous verbs violating guttural constraints (*Abā - Yaʾbā*) and defective double-kasrah verbs (*Wamiqa, Waritha, Waliyya*).
3. **The 20-Term Derivation Paradigm:**
   - Every door is traced across its complete derivational spectrum: *Māḍī, Muḍāriʿ, Maṣdar, Fāʿil, Majhūl, Mafʿūl, Jaḥd bi-Lam, Nafy bi-Lā, Nafy bi-Lan, Amr bi-Lām, Amr Ḥāḍir, Nahy, Nūn al-Tawkīd Thaqīlah/Khafīfah, Ism al-Zamān/Makān, Ism al-Ālah, Ism al-Marrah, Ism al-Nawʿ, Taṣghīr, Nisbah*.
4. **Morpho-Semantics of Augmentations (*Maʿānī Abwāb al-Ziyādah*):**
   - Systematic treatment of the semantic contributions of affixes: Transitivity (*Taʿdiyah*), Intransitive compliance (*Muṭāwaʿah*), Exaggeration (*Mubālaghah*), Inchoative state (*Ṣayrūrah*), and Pretension (*Taẓāhur*).
