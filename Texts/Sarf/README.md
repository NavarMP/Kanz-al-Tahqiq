## Edition status

The four Arabic files now contain first-pass OCR text for the identified work-specific page ranges. They are structured working transcriptions, not verified editions: diacritics, wording, tables, page transitions, and scan artifacts still require visual collation. The coverage ledger records the ranges and review state.

Keep the PDFs until every page and table has been checked and the final text has been reviewed. Commands and exercises printed in the source are part of the books and are transcribed as content.
