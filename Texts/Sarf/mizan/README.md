## 🌟 Editorial & Engineering Highlights

1. **Mandatory Complete Tashkīl (*Tashkīl al-Tāmm*):** Every single Arabic word, paradigm entry, and descriptive formula carries full vowel marks and inflectional case endings (*iʿrāb*).
2. **Traditional Dars Formulas Preserved:** Retains the authentic Indo-Persian scholastic formulas (`صِيغَةُ وَاحِدٍ مُذَكَّرٍ غَائِبٍ أَزْ فِعْلِ مَاضٍ مَعْرُوفٍ مُثْبَتٍ ضَمِير دَرْو هُوَ: فَعَلَ`).
3. **Decoupled Modern Drills:** Exercises from the modern commentary edition are systematically curated in `Notes/04_pedagogical_drills.md`, leaving `mizan.md` as an uncluttered classical Matn.
4. **Authoritative Web Linking:** Direct canonical citations to [Quran.com](https://quran.com) and [Shamela.ws](https://shamela.ws).
5. **Bidirectional Interactive Navigation:** Every paradigm table concludes with an instant jump link back to the Master Table of Contents.
