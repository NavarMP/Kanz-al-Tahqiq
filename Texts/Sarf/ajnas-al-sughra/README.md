# Kitāb al-Ajnās al-Ṣughrā (كتاب الأجناس الصغرى)
### *A Foundational Primer on Arabic Morphological Paradigms*
*Author: al-Shaykh Dādā ʿAlī ibn al-Shaykh Qāsim (d. 1127 AH / 1715 CE)*  
*Discipline: ʿIlm al-Ṣarf (Arabic Morphology) · Kanz al-Taḥqīq Library*

---

## Overview

**Kitāb al-Ajnās al-Ṣughrā** is the celebrated introductory morphological manual studied across traditional Islamic Dars curricula as the essential bridge between foundational conjugation drills (*al-Mīzān*) and advanced structural philology (*al-Ajnās al-Kubrā* and *al-Taṣrīf al-ʿIzzī*).

Composed by the 17th-century Baghdad-born philologist **Dādā ʿAlī ibn Qāsim**, this treatise classifies verbal patterns into unaugmented trilateral verbs (*Thulāthī Mujarrad*), augmented trilateral verbs (*Thulāthī Mazīd fīh*), quadrilateral roots (*Rubāʿī Mujarrad*), coordinated forms (*Mulḥaqāt*), and augmented quadrilaterals (*Rubāʿī Mazīd fīh*), concluding with the classification of roots based on phonetic soundness (*al-Aqsām al-Sabʿah*).

## Core Subject Matter

1. **The 5 Foundational Doors of the Unaugmented Trilateral:**
   - Door 1: *Faʿala - Yafʿilu* (ضَرَبَ - يَضْرِبُ)
   - Door 2: *Faʿala - Yafʿulu* (نَصَرَ - يَنْصُرُ)
   - Door 3: *Faʿala - Yafʿalu* (فَتَحَ - يَفْتَحُ)
   - Door 4: *Faʿil - Yafʿalu* (سَمِعَ - يَسْمَعُ)
   - Door 5: *Faʿula - Yafʿulu* (كَرُمَ - يَكْرُمُ)
2. **The 14 Augmented Trilateral Patterns:**
   - 1-Letter Augmentations: *Afʿala* (إِفْعَال), *Faʿʿala* (تَفْعِيل), *Fāʿala* (مُفَاعَلَة).
   - 2-Letter Augmentations: *Infaʿala* (انْفِعَال), *Iftaʿala* (افْتِعَال), *Ifʿalla* (افْعِلَال), *Tafaʿʿala* (تَفَعُّل), *Tafāʿala* (تَفَاعُل).
   - 3-Letter Augmentations: *Istafʿala* (اسْتِفْعَال), *Ifʿawʿala* (افْعِيعَال), *Ifʿawwala* (افْعِوَّال), *Ifʿālla* (افْعِيلَال).
3. **The Quadrilateral Spectrum:**
   - Base root: *Faʿlala* (دَحْرَجَ).
   - Coordinated (*Mulḥaq*): *Fawʿala, Fayʿala, Faʿwala, Faʿyala, Faʿlala, Faʿlā*.
   - Augmented: *Tafaʿlala, Ifʿanlala, Ifʿalalla*.
4. **The Seven Root Classes (*al-Aqsām al-Sabʿah*):**
   - *al-Ṣaḥīḥ, al-Mithāl, al-Ajwaf, al-Nāqiṣ, al-Lafīf al-Mafrūq, al-Lafīf al-Maqrūn, al-Muḍāʿaf*.
