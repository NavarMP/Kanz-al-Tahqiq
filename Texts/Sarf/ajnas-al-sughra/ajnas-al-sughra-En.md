## Introduction: The Genus of Verbs (*Ajnās al-Afʿāl*)

<div dir="rtl" lang="ar">
فَصْلٌ: فِي أَجْنَاسِ الْأَفْعَالِ: اعْلَمْ أَنَّ الْأَفْعَالَ: إِمَّا ثُلَاثِيٌّ، وَإِمَّا رُبَاعِيٌّ. وَكُلُّ وَاحِدٍ مِنْهُمَا: إِمَّا مُجَرَّدٌ، أَوْ مَزِيدٌ فِيهِ.
</div>

**Translation & Grammatical Exposition:**  
*Section on the Genus of Verbs:*  
Know that verbal roots in the Arabic language are fundamentally bipartite with respect to their radical consonants: they are either **trilateral (*thulāthī*)**, having three base consonants, or **quadrilateral (*rubāʿī*)**, having four base consonants. Each of these two categories is further bifurcated into either **unaugmented (*mujarrad*)**, consisting solely of root radicals, or **augmented (*mazīd fīh*)**, wherein one to three affixal morphemes are appended to the root consonants.

---

## Part I: The Unaugmented Trilateral Verbs (5 Classical Doors)

In the pedagogical framework of *al-Ajnās al-Ṣughrā*, the unaugmented trilateral verbs are organized into five primary operational gates (*abwāb*), determined by the vowel alternation of the medial radical (*ʿAyn al-Fiʿl*) between the perfective (*Māḍī*) and imperfective (*Muḍāriʿ*) stems:

### 1. Door I: *Faʿala - Yafʿilu* (فتـح - كسـر)
- **Base Paradigm:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">ضَرَبَ - يَضْرِبُ</span> (*ḍaraba - yaḍribu* - to strike).
- **Derivation Chain:**
  - Perfective Active: *ḍaraba* (ضَرَبَ)
  - Imperfective Active: *yaḍribu* (يَضْرِبُ)
  - Verbal Noun: *ḍarban* (ضَرْبًا)
  - Active Participle: *ḍāribun* (ضَارِبٌ)
  - Perfective Passive: *ḍuriba* (ضُرِبَ)
  - Imperfective Passive: *yuḍrabu* (يُضْرَبُ)
  - Passive Participle: *maḍrūbun* (مَضْرُوبٌ)
  - Imperative: *iḍrib* (اضْرِبْ)
  - Prohibitive: *lā taḍrib* (لَا تَضْرِبْ)

### 2. Door II: *Faʿala - Yafʿulu* (فتـح - ضـم)
- **Base Paradigm:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">نَصَرَ - يَنْصُرُ</span> (*naṣara - yanṣuru* - to help/assist).
- Imperative: *unṣur* (انْصُرْ); Prohibitive: *lā tanṣur* (لَا تَنْصُرْ).

### 3. Door III: *Faʿala - Yafʿalu* (فتحتـان)
- **Base Paradigm:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">فَتَحَ - يَفْتَحُ</span> (*fataḥa - yaftaḥu* - to open/conquer).
- Phonetic Constraint: Requires a guttural consonant (*Ḥurūf al-Ḥalq*: ء، هـ، ع، ح، غ، خ) at the medial or final radical position.
- Imperative: *iftaḥ* (افْتَحْ); Prohibitive: *lā taftaḥ* (لَا تَفْتَحْ).

### 4. Door IV: *Faʿila - Yafʿalu* (كسـر - فتـح)
- **Base Paradigm:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">سَمِعَ - يَسْمَعُ</span> (*samiʿa - yasmaʿu* - to hear).
- Imperative: *ismaʿ* (اسْمَعْ); Prohibitive: *lā tasmaʿ* (لَا تَسْمَعْ).

### 5. Door V: *Faʿula - Yafʿulu* (ضمَّتـان)
- **Base Paradigm:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">كَرُمَ - يَكْرُمُ</span> (*karuma - yakrumu* - to be noble/generous).
- Syntactic Constraint: Strictly intransitive (*Lāzim*), conveying inherent character traits, dispositions, or permanent attributes. Neither passive forms nor passive participles are derived from this gate.
- Imperative: *ukrum* (اكْرُمْ); Prohibitive: *lā takrum* (لَا تَكْرُمْ).

## Part II: The Augmented Trilateral Verbs (14 Doors)

The 14 augmented trilateral patterns are systematically grouped by the quantity of appended prefixal, infixal, or geminate letters:

### 1. Group A: One-Letter Augmentations (Forms II, III, IV)
- **Bāb al-Ifʿāl:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">أَفْعَلَ يُفْعِلُ إِفْعَالًا</span> (*akrama - yukrimu - ikrāman*). Primarily transitivizing (*Taʿdiyah*).
- **Bāb al-Tafʿīl:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">فَعَّلَ يُفَعِّلُ تَفْعِيلًا</span> (*farraḥa - yufarriḥu - tafrīḥan*). Denotes intensification and causativity.
- **Bāb al-Mufāʿalah:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">فَاعَلَ يُفَاعِلُ مُفَاعَلَةً</span> (*qātala - yuqātilu - muqātalah*). Denotes reciprocal action between two parties.

### 2. Group B: Two-Letter Augmentations (Forms V, VI, VII, VIII, IX)
- **Bāb al-Infiʿāl:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">انْفَعَلَ يَنْفَعِلُ انْفِعَالًا</span> (*inkasara - yankasiru*). Strictly intransitive and reflexive (*Muṭāwaʿah*).
- **Bāb al-Iftiʿāl:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">افْتَعَلَ يَفْتَعِلُ افْتِعَالًا</span> (*ijtamaʿa - yajtamiʿu*). Denotes effort, acquisition, and reflexivity.
- **Bāb al-Ifʿilāl:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">افْعَلَّ يَفْعَلُّ افْعِلَالًا</span> (*iḥmarra - yaḥmarru*). Conveys intensification of colours and sensory flaws.
- **Bāb al-Tafaʿʿul:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">تَفَعَّلَ يَتَفَعَّلُ تَفَعُّلًا</span> (*taʿallama - yataʿallamu*). Denotes gradual, conscious exertion (*Takalluf*).
- **Bāb al-Tafāʿul:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">تَفَاعَلَ يَتَفَاعَلُ تَفَاعُلًا</span> (*tabāʿada - yatabāʿadu*). Denotes mutual participation or pretense (*Taẓāhur*).

### 3. Group C: Three-Letter Augmentations (Forms X, XI, XII, XIII)
- **Bāb al-Istifʿāl:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">اسْتَفْعَلَ يَسْتَفْعِلُ اسْتِفْعَالًا</span> (*istakhraja - yastakhriju*). Conveys petition/request (*Ṭalab*) or transformation (*Ṣayrūrah*).
- **Bāb al-Ifʿīʿāl:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">افْعَوْعَلَ يَفْعَوْعِلُ افْعِيعَالًا</span> (*iʿshawshaba - yaʿshawshibu*). Expresses extreme qualitative exuberance.
- **Bāb al-Ifʿiwwāl:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">افْعَوَّلَ يَفْعَوِّلُ افْعِوَّالًا</span> (*ijlawwadha - yajlawwidhu*). Indicates accelerated, continuous velocity.
- **Bāb al-Ifʿīlāl:** <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">افْعَالَّ يَفْعَالُّ افْعِيلَالًا</span> (*iḥmārra - yaḥmārru*). Intensive permanent pigmentation.

## Part III: The Quadrilateral Root, Coordinated Verbs, and Augmented Quadrilaterals

1. **Unaugmented Quadrilateral:**  
   Single base gate: <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">فَعْلَلَ يُفَعْلِلُ</span> (*daḥraja - yudaḥriju* - to roll).
2. **Coordinated Quadrilaterals (*Mulḥaqāt*):**  
   Trilateral roots expanded by a resonant consonant to replicate the metrical balance of *daḥraja*:
   - *Fawʿala* (*ḥawqala*), *Fayʿala* (*bayṭara*), *Faʿwala* (*jahwara*), *Faʿyala* (*ʿathyara*), *Faʿlala* (*jalbaba*), *Faʿlā* (*salqā*).
3. **Augmented Quadrilaterals:**
   - 1-Letter Augmentation: <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">تَفَعْلَلَ يَتَفَعْلَلُ</span> (*tadaḥraja* - reflexive rolling).
   - 2-Letter Augmentation: <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">افْعَنْلَلَ</span> (*iḥranjama* - to flock together), <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">افْعَلَلَّ</span> (*iqshaʿarra* - to shudder/tremble).

## Part IV: The Seven Phonetic Classes (*al-Aqsām al-Sabʿah*)

Verbs are categorized into seven classes based on radical composition and susceptibility to morphological mutation:
1. **Sound (*al-Ṣaḥīḥ*):** Completely free from weak semivowels (*wāw, yāʾ*), hamzah, or gemination (e.g. *naṣara, ḍaraba*).
2. **Assimilated (*al-Mithāl*):** Initial radical is weak (e.g. *waʿada, yasara*).
3. **Hollow (*al-Ajwaf*):** Medial radical is weak (e.g. *qāla, bāʿa*).
4. **Defective (*al-Nāqiṣ*):** Final radical is weak (e.g. *daʿā, ramā*).
5. **Separated Involute (*al-Lafīf al-Mafrūq*):** Initial and final radicals are weak, separated by a sound medial radical (e.g. *waqā, wafā*).
6. **Adjacent Involute (*al-Lafīf al-Maqrūn*):** Medial and final radicals are weak consecutively (e.g. *ṭawā, shawā*).
7. **Geminate (*al-Muḍāʿaf*):** Medial and final radicals are identical homorganic consonants (e.g. *madda, farra*).
