# Oral Arts Taxonomy & Production Guidelines

This document specifies the acoustic pacing, vocal modulation, and structural conventions for each oral and poetic art form preserved in the Kanz al-Taḥqīq repository.

---

## 1. Oratorical Speech (*Khuṭbah / Waʿẓ / Fest Oratory*)

- **Target Pace:** 105–125 Words Per Minute (WPM).
- **Structure:**
  1. **Exordium (*Ibtidā’*):** Solemn, hushed opening with Basmalah, Hamdalah, and Salawat. Pauses of 800–1200ms between invocations.
  2. **Fest Salutation:** Respectful, dignified address to judges, teachers, and audience.
  3. **Exposition:** Clear historical or philosophical contextualization.
  4. **Sacred Evidences:** Quranic and Hadith proofs, chanted or recited with Tajwīd reverence.
  5. **Oratorical Climax:** Gradual crescendo; rising volume, vocal chest resonance, and rhythmic moral antithesis.
  6. **Peroration & Benediction:** Decrescendo, peaceful resolution, and traditional Islamic Salam.
- **Acoustic Atmosphere:** "Sanctuary / Lecture Hall" reverberation (decay ~1.2s, wet level -22 dB).

---

## 2. Formal Debate (*Munāḍharah*)

- **Target Pace:** 135–155 Words Per Minute (WPM).
- **Structure:**
  1. **Opening Statement:** Rapid framing of thesis, demarcation of terms, and presentation of primary syllogisms.
  2. **Intervention / Question Rounds:** Crisp, interrogative pitch inflection.
  3. **Rebuttal Vault (*Radd*):** Sharp, punchy deconstruction of opponent fallacies.
  4. **Closing Statement:** Synthesis of uncontroverted evidence and final verdict call.
- **Debate Stage SFX:**
  - **4:00 Warning Bell:** Single soft brass chime to alert speaker of 1 minute remaining.
  - **5:00 Termination Bell:** Double brass chime indicating hard conclusion.
- **Acoustic Atmosphere:** Direct, punchy broadcast sound with higher compression ratio (4:1) and tighter room acoustics to maximize syllogistic aggression and intelligibility.

---

## 3. Metric Poetry (*Shiʿr / Qasīdah / Mappila Pāṭṭu*)

- **Target Pace:** Governed strictly by poetic meter (*Bahr* / *Vṛttam*), typically 80–100 WPM.
- **Key Conventions:**
  - **Caesura (*ʿArūḍ*):** Enforce a 500–700ms pause at the midpoint of each couplet (*Bayt*).
  - **Rhyme Ending (*Rawiyy*):** Lengthen the final vowel sound by 300ms before moving to the next verse.
  - **Stanza Intervals:** 1200–1500ms pause between stanzas.
- **Acoustic Atmosphere:** Subtle melodic drone (Ney, Tanpura, or ethereal pad) keyed to the recitation root note, ducked smoothly beneath the voice.

---

## 4. Dars Fest Manuals & Administrative Proclamations

- **Target Pace:** 120–130 WPM.
- **Conventions:**
  - Crisp, dry acoustic environment (zero reverb).
  - High intelligibility with high-frequency clarity boost.
  - Distinct itemized numbering pauses (600ms after rule numbers).
