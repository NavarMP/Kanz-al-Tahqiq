# Trilingual Terminology Map (معجم المصطلحات المعتمدة)

This reference defines the authoritative technical vocabulary across the four core disciplines of **Kanz al-Taḥqīq**: **Manṭiq** (Logic), **Fiqh** (Jurisprudence), **Naḥw** (Grammar), and **Balāghah** (Rhetoric).

---

## 1. Manṭiq (Traditional Islamic Logic)

| Arabic Headword | Transliteration | English Technical Equivalent | Malayalam Madrasa Term | Contextual Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **التَّصَوُّر** | al-Taṣawwur | Conception / Simple Apprehension | തസ്വവ്വുർ | Grasping an essence or meaning without attributing an affirmative or negative judgment. |
| **التَّصْدِيق** | al-Taṣdīq | Propositional Assent / Judgment | തസ്ദീഖ് | Grasping an essence together with an affirmation or denial of a relation. |
| **الدَّلَالَة** | al-Dalālah | Signification / Indication | ദലാലത്ത് | The property whereby knowledge of one thing (sign) leads to knowledge of another. |
| **الدَّلَالَةُ الْوَضْعِيَّةُ** | al-Dalālah al-Waḍʿiyyah | Conventional Signification | വദ്ഈ ദലാലത്ത് | Signification established by human or societal linguistic convention. |
| **الْمُطَابَقَة** | al-Muṭābaqah | Complete Coincidence (Correspondence) | മുതവാബഖത്ത് | Signification of an expression for its entire conventional meaning. |
| **التَّضَمُّن** | al-Taḍammun | Containment / Inclusion | തളമ്മുൻ | Signification of an expression for a component part of its meaning. |
| **الِالْتِزَام** | al-Iltizām | Implication / Necessary Concomitance | ഇൽതിസാം | Signification of an expression for an external inseparable attribute. |
| **الْكُلِّيّ** | al-Kullī | Universal Concept | കുല്ലി | A concept whose mental representation does not preclude multiple instances. |
| **الْجُزْئِيّ** | al-Juz'ī | Particular Concept | ജുസ്ഇ | A concept that by its nature can only refer to a single individual. |
| **الْكُلِّيَّاتُ الْخَمْس** | al-Kulliyyāt al-Khams | The Five Universals (Quinque Voces) | കുല്ലിയ്യാത്തുൽ ഖംസ് | Jins (Genus), Nawʿ (Species), Faṣl (Differentia), Khāṣṣah (Property), ʿAraḍ ʿĀmm (Accident). |
| **الْقَوْلُ الشَّارِح** | al-Qawl al-Shāriḥ | Definition / Explanatory Phrase | ഖൗലുൻ ശാരിഹ് | An arrangement of universals that reveals the essence of an unknown concept. |
| **الْقَضِيَّة** | al-Qaḍiyyah | Proposition | ഖദിയ്യ | A declarative statement that can be judged true or false. |
| **الْمَوْضُوع** | al-Mawḍūʿ | Subject (of a proposition) | മൗദൂഅ് | That about which a predicate is affirmed or denied. |
| **الْمَحْمُول** | al-Maḥmūl | Predicate (of a proposition) | മഹ്മൂൽ | The attribute affirmed or denied of the subject. |
| **الْقِيَاس** | al-Qiyās | Syllogism | ഖിയാസ് | A composite argument wherein the premises necessitate a distinct conclusion. |
| **الْبُرْهَان** | al-Burhān | Demonstration / Apodeictic Proof | ബുർഹാൻ | A syllogism composed exclusively of certain, necessary premises (*Yaqīniyyāt*). |

## 2. Fiqh (Shafi'i Jurisprudence — Fatḥ al-Muʿīn)

| Arabic Headword | Transliteration | English Equivalent | Malayalam Term | Contextual Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **الْحُكْمُ الشَّرْعِيّ** | al-Ḥukm al-Sharʿī | Sharia Legal Norm | ശർഈ വിധി | Communication from Allah related to human actions as demand, option, or condition. |
| **الْوَاجِب / الْفَرْض** | al-Wājib / al-Farḍ | Obligatory Act | വാജിബ് / ഫർള് | An act whose execution is rewarded and whose willful omission incurs liability. |
| **الْمَنْدُوب / السُّنَّة** | al-Mandūb / al-Sunnah | Recommended / Sunnah | സുന്നത്ത് / മന്ദൂബ് | An act whose execution is commended but whose omission is not punished. |
| **الْحَرَام** | al-Ḥarām | Prohibited Act | ഹറാം | An act strictly forbidden by definitive divine command. |
| **الْمَكْرُوه** | al-Makrūh | Disliked Act | മക്റൂഹ് | An act discourged by the Lawgiver without punishment for doing it. |
| **الْمُبَاح** | al-Mubāḥ | Permissible / Neutral | മുബാഹ് | An act left to the legal agent's discretion with neither praise nor blame. |
| **الشَّرْط** | al-Sharṭ | Condition Precedent | ശർത്ത് | An extrinsic requisite without which an act is invalid, but whose presence does not necessitate validity. |
| **الرُّكْن** | al-Rukn | Essential Element / Pillar | റുക്ൻ | An intrinsic constitutive component of a devotional act (e.g., Fatihah in prayer). |
| **الْمَانِع** | al-Māniʿ | Impediment | മാനിഅ് | An extrinsic factor whose presence invalidates an act (e.g., ritual impurity). |
| **الْحَدَثُ الْأَصْغَر** | al-Ḥadath al-Aṣghar | Minor Ritual Impurity | ചെറിയ അശുദ്ധി | A state requiring Wudhu (ablution). |
| **الْحَدَثُ الْأَكْبَر** | al-Ḥadath al-Akbar | Major Ritual Impurity | വലിയ അശുദ്ധി | A state requiring Ghusl (full ritual bath). |
| **النَّجَاسَة** | al-Najāsah | Physical Ritual Filth | നജസ് | Impure matter that prevents the validity of prayer. |

## 3. Naḥw (Arabic Grammar & Syntax — Alfiyyah)

| Arabic Headword | Transliteration | English Equivalent | Malayalam Term | Contextual Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **الْإِعْرَاب** | al-Iʿrāb | Desinential Inflection | ഇഅ്റാബ് | Change in word endings caused by governing agents (*ʿAwāmil*). |
| **الْبِنَاء** | al-Bināʾ | Non-inflection / Fixity | ബിനാഅ് | Invariable constancy of a word ending regardless of governing agents. |
| **الْمَرْفُوعَات** | al-Marfūʿāt | Nominative Case Forms | മർഫൂആത്ത് | Grammatical roles taking Dammah (e.g. Fāʿil, Mubtada', Khabar). |
| **الْمَنْصُوبَات** | al-Manṣūbāt | Accusative Case Forms | മൻസൂബാത്ത് | Grammatical roles taking Fathah (e.g. Mafʿūl, Ḥāl, Tamyīz). |
| **الْمَجْرُورَات** | al-Majrūrāt | Genitive Case Forms | മജ്റൂറാത്ത് | Words governed by prepositions or annexations (Iḍāfah). |
| **الْمَجْزُومَات** | al-Majzūmāt | Jussive Forms | മജ്സൂമാത്ത് | Imperfect verbs governed by apocopating particles (e.g. Lam, In). |
| **الْإِضَافَة** | al-Iḍāfah | Genitive Annexation | ഇളാഫത്ത് | Construct state linking Muḍāf and Muḍāf Ilayh. |

## 4. Balāghah (Arabic Rhetoric — Qawāʿid al-Balāghah)

| Arabic Headword | Transliteration | English Equivalent | Malayalam Term | Contextual Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **الْفَصَاحَة** | al-Faṣāḥah | Clarity / Eloquence | ഫസ്വാഹത്ത് | Purity of words from harsh phonetics (*Tanāfur*), archaic oddity (*Gharābah*), or grammatical solecism. |
| **الْبَلَاغَة** | al-Balāghah | Rhetoric / Appropriateness | ബലാഗത്ത് | Conforming an eloquent speech to the situational context and audience requirements (*Muqtaḍā al-Ḥāl*). |
| **عِلْمُ الْمَعَانِي** | ʿIlm al-Maʿānī | Semantics & Pragmatics | ഇൽമുൽ മആനി | Study of syntactic patterns to convey contextual shades of meaning. |
| **عِلْمُ الْبَيَان** | ʿIlm al-Bayān | Figures of Speech / Imagery | ഇൽമുൽ ബയാൻ | Methods of expressing a single thought in varying degrees of clarity (Tashbīh, Majāz, Kināyah). |
| **عِلْمُ الْبَدِيع** | ʿIlm al-Badīʿ | Embellishment / Ornaments | ഇൽമുൽ ബദീഅ് | Stylistic ornaments, both verbal (*Lafẓī*) such as Jinās, and semantic (*Maʿnawī*) such as Ṭibāq. |
| **الِاسْتِعَارَة** | al-Istiʿārah | Metaphor | ഇസ്തിആറ | A figurative substitution based on similarity where one of the two poles of simile is deleted. |
| **الْكِنَايَة** | al-Kināyah | Metonymy / Allusion | കിനായത്ത് | An expression implying an associated meaning while still permitting the literal sense. |
