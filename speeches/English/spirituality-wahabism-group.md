# Spirituality Is Far from Wahabism

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">السلام عليكم ورحمة الله</p>

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">بسم الله والحمد لله والصلاة والسلام على رسول الله وعلى آله وصحبه أجمعين، أما بعد</p>

Respected teachers, honourable judges, and my dear brothers,

Today, I stand before you to speak on a vital topic: **“Spirituality Is Far from Wahabism.”**

Islam is not just about outer rules. It is a divine system that illuminates our hearts and purifies our souls. Allah Almighty says in the Holy Qur’an:

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَن زَكَّاهَا وَقَدْ خَابَ مَن دَسَّاهَا</p>

Which means: *“Successful indeed is the one who purifies his soul, and ruined is the one who corrupts it.”* This is the true foundation of Islamic spirituality: **Tazkiyatun Nafs**. But the Wahhabi ideology focused heavily on literal rules and opposed many beautiful spiritual traditions preserved by traditional Sunni scholars.

Let us look at four key points:

## First: The Love and Honour of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ)

For Ahl al-Sunnah, loving the Prophet is the core of our faith. We express this through Salawat and gatherings of Mawlid. Yet, Wahhabi ideology labels Mawlid as a bid’ah. But we ask: how can remembering the greatest blessing of Allah ever be wrong? Allah says: <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">قُلْ بِفَضْلِ اللَّهِ وَبِرَحْمَتِهِ فَبِذَٰلِكَ فَلْيَفْرَحُوا</span> — *“In the bounty of Allah and in His mercy, in that let them rejoice.”*

## Second: Tawassul and Honouring the Awliya

Sunni scholars permit Tawassul, believing firmly that Allah alone gives everything. Allah commands: <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا اتَّقُوا اللَّهَ وَابْتَغُوا إِلَيْهِ الْوَسِيلَةَ</span>. However, Wahhabism quickly labels Tawassul as Shirk. My brothers, respecting a servant of Allah is not worshipping him! Honouring the Awliya never breaks Tawhid!

## Third: Visiting the Graves (Ziyarat)

Our beloved Prophet (ﷺ) commanded: <span dir="rtl" lang="ar">كُنْتُ نَهَيْتُكُمْ عَنْ زِيَارَةِ الْقُبُورِ فَزُورُوهَا</span> — *“Visit the graves.”* Visiting graves reminds us of death and the hereafter. We do not worship graves; we only pray to Allah while honouring His righteous servants.

## Fourth: The Essence of Tasawwuf

Authentic Tasawwuf is not deviation; it is the science of purifying the heart from pride, jealousy, and arrogance. Great masters like Imam Junayd al-Baghdadi and Imam al-Ghazali showed us that Shariah and spirituality go hand in hand. Yet, Wahhabi ideology completely rejected this spiritual heritage.

Our Prophet (ﷺ) defined Ihsan as:

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">أَنْ تَعْبُدَ اللَّهَ كَأَنَّكَ تَرَاهُ، فَإِنْ لَمْ تَكُنْ تَرَاهُ فَإِنَّهُ يَرَاكَ</p>

*“To worship Allah as though you see Him; and if you do not see Him, know that He sees you.”* This is the true soul of spirituality! What is the use of worship if the heart has no sincerity?

My dear brothers, we believe in Tawhid without insulting the Prophet. We believe in Shariah without abandoning Tasawwuf. A religion that touches only the body but forgets the heart has lost its true beauty.

May Allah bless us with clean hearts, deep love for our Prophet, and the sweetness of Ihsan.

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">وَآخِرُ دَعْوَانَا أَنِ الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ</p>

Assalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullah.
